Frequently asked questions

Tidewell is a fictional demo brand, so these answers describe how a real store might work.

Is this a real store?

No. Tidewell Ceramics is a demo project. You can browse, fill a cart and place a demo order, but nothing is charged, shipped or stored.

Are the pieces dishwasher and microwave safe?

Yes. Every piece is high-fired stoneware that is safe in the dishwasher, microwave and oven up to 220 C. Avoid sudden temperature changes, such as moving a dish from the freezer to a hot oven.

Are the glazes food safe?

Yes. All glazes are lead-free and tested for food contact.

Why does my piece look slightly different from the photo?

We mix glazes in small batches, so colour and pooling vary a little from piece to piece. This is normal and is part of handmade ceramics.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is a flat $8 and is free on orders of $100 or more. Orders usually ship within three working days.

What is your returns policy?

You can return unused pieces in their original packaging within 30 days. If a piece arrives damaged, contact us with a photo and we will send a replacement.

What if a piece chips?

We replace any piece that chips within ten years of normal use.

Can I buy a single piece or must I buy a set?

You can buy any piece on its own. Every shape fits every glaze, so you can build a set over time.

How do I contact you?

Use the contact form or email hello@tidewell.example.
