About Tidewell

A small studio making stoneware for everyday tables. (Tidewell is a fictional brand created for a demo project.)

Our story

Tidewell started in a shared garage in Portland, with one second-hand kiln and a stack of test tiles. We wanted plates that felt good in the hand, survived the dishwasher and still looked good after five years.

Today a team of eight throws, glazes and fires every piece in the same studio. We make in batches of about forty, so each glaze comes out slightly different, and we think that is the point.

What we believe

Use it every day

Tableware should live on the table, not in a cabinet. Everything we make goes safely in the oven, microwave and dishwasher.

Fewer, better pieces

We make six shapes in four glazes. Every shape matches every glaze, so you can build a set slowly.

Made to last

Our stoneware is fired at 1,260 C until it is dense and hard-wearing. If a piece chips in the first ten years, we replace it.
