Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026. This is a sample policy for a fictional demo site.

Overview

Tidewell Ceramics is a demonstration website. It has no accounts, no payment processing and no database. This page explains what the demo does and does not do with your information.

What stays in your browser

Your shopping cart is saved in your browser's local storage so it survives a page refresh. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it at any time from the cart page or by clearing your browser data.

Forms

The contact form and the checkout form are simulated. What you type is checked in your browser and then discarded. Nothing is sent to a server, emailed or stored.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when a page loads. The site is hosted on Amazon Web Services, which keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and requested pages, for operating and securing the service.

Your choices

Because the demo collects no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. If this were a live store, this section would explain how to exercise those rights.
